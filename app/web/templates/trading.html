Interhub trading

Help & assumptions

All items with saved prices at both selected stations are considered. This is independent of the manufacturing catalogue.

Leave both hubs unselected to compare all 20 directions between five hubs. Select one hub to limit either purchases or sales. Same-hub trades are excluded. Rows are alternative routes; stock shared by different routes cannot be purchased twice. Default: buy from sell orders, sell into buy orders. Quantity is capped by stock at the lowest purchase price, profitable buy demand in your selected price range and your budget/cargo limits. More expensive source sell orders are excluded.

Routes use stargates only, without player shortcuts. Safe routes are checked for security 0.5 or higher in every system; shortest routes may cross dangerous space. Route data refreshes daily. Profit / jump = net profit for the entire batch / jumps, without travel costs or losses.

Buy demand uses actual price levels, not the best price for the entire batch. Unit profit uses the best quoted sale price; batch profit uses the weighted prices.

Price spikes only compares the current selected sale quote with the median of regional daily average prices over the last 7 completed UTC days. At least 3 days with trades and a positive average are required; missing or insufficient history is excluded. This is a regional benchmark, not a historical station order price. The threshold defaults to 30%; the filter is off by default.

Daily turnover uses the entire destination hub region, not the station: sum of daily average price times traded units, divided by the selected number of completed UTC days. Missing history is unknown, not zero; items without history are excluded when Filter daily turnover is enabled and a minimum is set. The toggle is off by default; turning it off preserves the entered minimum.

Buying with buy orders or selling with sell orders is a scenario. Orders must fill; sell-side stock is not demand. No selling-time estimate is implied.

Return on cost = net profit / purchase and procurement costs. Taxes default to zero. Buy broker applies only to purchase buy orders; sell broker only to sale sell orders; sales tax applies to both sale types.

Transport uses packaged volume when supplied by EVE Ref, otherwise item volume. Profit per m³ = net batch profit / batch cargo volume; without a specified batch, unit profit / item volume. This measures profit density, not a hauling tariff. Missing volumes show no density and are excluded only when a cargo limit needs them. Metadata refreshes weekly in the background. Listing changes, minimum order quantities and hauling losses are not simulated.
