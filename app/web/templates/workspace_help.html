Tax & calculation notes

All five rates default to 0%; blank means 0%. Buy broker applies to material buy orders, sell broker to product sell orders, and sales tax to either sale type. Facility tax and system cost index apply to manufacturing EIV, not market material prices. SCC surcharge remains included separately. Invention uses shared tax-free material estimates; these rates never create separate invention jobs.

How to read these results
Cost per unit
Materials, estimated job installation and optional invention/procurement fees for one finished item.
Profit per unit
Product price minus production costs and your selected sales tax/broker fees.
Margin
Profit divided by material cost, multiplied by 100. It is not a percentage of the sale price.
Profit per day
Theoretical production through one slot for 24 hours. Selling into buy orders caps this figure by available units and the actual buy prices within your selected range.
Profit on demand
Net profit from filling buy orders within the selected percentage below the best buy price, using each price level. Volumes smaller than a full production run, orders outside the range and future demand are excluded. Available only when product prices use buy orders.
Regional turnover
Estimated daily ISK traded across the entire hub region, averaged over your selected completed days. This is not your expected revenue or station-only demand.
Stale
One or more inputs are due for refresh: market snapshots after 20 minutes, regional history after 24 hours, recipes and included invention estimates at their saved refresh deadlines. The result uses the last available data. Orders refresh every 20 minutes; history and invention valuations refresh daily. Show results applies your settings to saved data.
